How to Use the Player

  • ▶
    Play / Pause

    Click the center button to start or pause playback. Clicking any track title will also begin playing it.

  • ⇄
    Skip Tracks

    Use the left and right arrow buttons to jump to the previous or next track in the playlist.

  • ▬
    Progress Bar

    Click anywhere on the progress bar to seek to that position. Click and drag to scrub through the track in real time.

  • ◎
    Volume Knob

    Click and drag upward to raise the volume, or downward to lower it. A single click without dragging resets the level to 0 dB.

  • ⇋
    Dry / Wet Toggle

    On the Analog Processing player, use this toggle to switch between the unprocessed (Dry) and processed (Wet) signal for a direct A/B comparison.

  • ☍
    Analyze

    Runs a full offline analysis on the currently loaded track. Results include integrated LUFS, true peak, dynamic range, BPM, key, spectral character, and stereo image. Tap ANALYZE with a track loaded — analysis runs in a background pass and may take a few seconds for longer files.

    Platform targets:
    Spotify / YouTube   –14 LUFS / <–1.0 dBTP
    Apple Music        –16 LUFS / <–1.0 dBTP
    Tidal              –14 LUFS / <–1.0 dBTP
    Amazon Music       –14 LUFS / <–2.0 dBTP
    Podcast            –16 LUFS / <–1.0 dBTP
    EBU R128 Broadcast  –23 LUFS / <–1.0 dBTP
    ATSC A/85 (US TV)   –24 LUFS / <–2.0 dBTP
