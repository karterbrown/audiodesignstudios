How to Use the Demo Player

  • ▶
    Play / Pause

    Click the center transport button to start or stop playback. Clicking a track title in the list will also begin playing it immediately.

  • ⇋
    Raw / Mix Toggle

    Switch between the unprocessed (Raw) and fully mixed (Mix) version of the track in real time. The VU meter reflects the active signal.

  • ▬
    Progress Bar

    Click anywhere on the progress bar to jump to that position, or click and drag to scrub through the track.

  • ◎
    Volume Knob

    Drag upward to raise the volume, downward to lower it. A single click without dragging resets the level to 0 dB.

  • 📊
    Analyze

    Runs a full loudness and dynamics analysis on the current track with K-weighting (ITU-R BS.1770-4 standard). Reports accurate LUFS (Loudness Units relative to Full Scale), Sample Peak, and LRA (Loudness Range) values for streaming platform compliance. Note: Sample Peak measures the highest digital sample value; True Peak (dBTP) would require 4x oversampling for inter-sample peak detection.

  • 👁
    View

    Opens the real-time spectrum analyzer and metering display. Shows short-term Peak, RMS approximations, and dynamic range while playing. For accurate, full-track LUFS analysis, use the Analyze button.
